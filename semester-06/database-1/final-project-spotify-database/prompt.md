# Database I Final Project — Spotify-Like Platform

The final project asked teams to design and implement a relational database for a simplified Spotify-like music platform.

## Core database scope

The design was expected to model users, singers, songs, albums, playlists, comments, likes, friendships, followers, messages, wallets, concerts, and tickets. Students were expected to define the required tables and relationships and to use database concepts taught in the course, including procedures/functions where appropriate.

## User-side requirements

Users could have basic profile information and subscription state. Subscribers were expected to have richer platform capabilities such as liking and commenting on music content, receiving recommendations based on interests, managing friendships and messages, following other users and singers, maintaining a wallet, purchasing concert tickets, managing favorites, searching music, creating public/private playlists, and viewing lyrics.

## Singer-side requirements

Singer accounts were expected to support adding songs and albums, restricting playlist addition where needed, publishing concerts and ticket prices, deleting songs/albums from availability, and cancelling concerts with ticket refunds.

## Deliverables and design expectations

- Complete ER diagram
- Relational database implementation
- A programming-language-based user menu, with Python suggested by the assignment
- Attention to relational design quality, especially avoiding unnecessary redundancy and inconsistency
- Teams of up to three students
